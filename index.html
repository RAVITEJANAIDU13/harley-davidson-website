ADVANCED MOTOR DIVISION // TELEMETRY

POWER MEETS
PRECISION.

Liquid cooling, structural chassis engineering, and cornering-aware rider safety technology combined with visceral American V-Twin soul.

POWERTRAIN DYNAMICS

ENGINEERING

Revolution Max 1250T DOHC liquid-cooled V-Twin architecture with dual independent Variable Valve Timing. 121 horsepower with surging low-end torque.

STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY

DESIGN

Stressed-member engine chassis eliminating traditional frame hoops. Inverted 43mm cartridge forks and radial Brembo monobloc braking calipers.

HUMAN-MACHINE INTERFACE

RIDER EXPERIENCE

Full-color 4.0-inch TFT cockpit display with turn-by-turn navigation HUD, 6-axis IMU-assisted Cornering ABS, and selectable ride modes.
